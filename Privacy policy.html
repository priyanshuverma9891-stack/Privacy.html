1. Introduction

Welcome to Gynie AI ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our web or mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

  • Profile Information: Name, Gender, and your selected Local Hub (Gym/Clinic).
  • Fitness Goals: Your chosen AI persona (e.g., Show-off, Chill, Discipline) to customize your coaching experience.
  • Workout Data: Rep counts, form accuracy, and exercise type.

3. Camera & Video Data (Important)

Gynie AI requires camera access for the "Smart-Set Auto-Clip" and real-time rep counting feature (powered by Vision AI).

Privacy Guarantee:

  • Your camera feed is processed in real-time to track body joints and angles.
  • We DO NOT record, store, or upload your workout videos to our servers.
  • All visual processing happens instantly for AI coaching and is discarded immediately.

4. Payments & E-commerce

If you purchase fitness equipment (e.g., Resistance Bands) through our app, payments (such as ₹50 COD advances) are processed securely via third-party UPI applications. We do not collect, store, or process your bank account details, UPI PINs, or credit card information.

5. How We Use Your Information

  • To generate contextual AI audio feedback (roasts/motivation) using third-party AI providers.
  • To deliver purchased physical items to your selected "Local Hub".
  • To improve our rep-counting algorithms.
